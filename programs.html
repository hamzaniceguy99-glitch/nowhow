Program 01 · Advice, then work

IT consultancy

Most IT problems presented as technical turn out to be decisions nobody made: what the system is actually for, who owns it, what happens when it stops. We look at the estate, say what we would change and in what order, and put a cost against each. Where a decision belongs with you rather than with us, we will say so rather than quietly making it, because a supplier who decides things for a client ends up owning them in ways neither party intended.

Program 02 · You stay controller

Managed systems

Running your systems means handling the personal data in them, so the processing agreement comes before the credentials. You remain the controller, we act as your processor, no sub-processor is engaged without your authorisation, and at the end we return or delete the data at your choice rather than ours. Backups are only real if a restore has been tested, so we test them. We make no claim that any system is secure against all attack.

Program 03 · Not a legal service

Roles and agreements

The roles follow from who decides what data is used for, not from what a contract says, and a processor that starts deciding purposes becomes a controller for that processing. So the written agreement records what is actually happening. It also sets the breach route: a controller has seventy-two hours to tell the supervisory authority, a processor must tell the controller without undue delay, so we tell you at once because your clock starts then. We are not lawyers and this is not legal advice.

  • Written agreement
  • Breach route agreed

Program 04 · Your board, not ours

NIS2 readiness

The Belgian rules transposing the second network and information security directive came into force in October 2024 and expressly cover providers of managed services. Reporting runs to a tight rhythm: an early warning within twenty-four hours, a notification within seventy-two, a final report within a month. The part clients miss is governance, because the management body has to approve and supervise the measures and can be liable for failing to. That accountability is your board's and cannot be outsourced to us.
